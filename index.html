Italian bakery culture, made modern.

Sapori Lab creates modern Italian bakery drops inspired by traditional flavours and designed for today’s food culture. From cannoli kits and tiramisù trays to focaccia slabs and curated bakery boxes, everything is produced fresh in limited quantities every week.
